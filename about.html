About the project

Explore the stories behind Australia

A Window into Australia is a simple archive exploration interface designed to help people discover Australia's audiovisual history through time, people and places.

The experience prioritises curiosity rather than requiring users to know exactly what they are looking for. Users can browse by era or medium, search the archive, open detailed items, and discover unexpected material through Surprise Me.

A browsable view of archival film, audio, images and documentary material.
